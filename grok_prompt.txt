# MASTER PROMPT — BUILD A WORLD-CLASS BC LANDLORD PLATFORM

## ROLE

Act as a **world-class website designer, UX/UI architect, product strategist, cybersecurity-minded engineer, and membership-platform specialist**.

You are designing and building a premium digital platform for **landlords and rental-property owners in British Columbia, Canada**.

This is NOT supposed to look like a traditional association website.

The goal is to create a platform that feels:

* Modern
* Trustworthy
* Professional
* Premium
* Extremely easy to use
* Fast
* Secure
* Mobile-first
* Community-driven
* Data-conscious
* Built specifically for BC landlords

The website should feel like a **modern technology platform combined with a professional membership organization**, rather than a government website or an outdated industry association.

The domain name is already registered and will be connected to the finished website.

---

# 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create the leading online platform where **BC landlords can join a community, access useful resources, share experiences, understand their rights and responsibilities, document tenant-related issues, obtain educational information, and collectively communicate concerns affecting rental-property owners.**

The platform should make a landlord think:

> "This is the place I go when I have a rental-property problem."

The platform should serve:

* First-time landlords
* Small landlords with one rental unit
* Basement-suite landlords
* Condo landlords
* Owners of multiple rental properties
* Experienced landlords
* Property managers
* Rental-housing professionals

The website must NOT encourage harassment, retaliation, discrimination, doxxing, naming-and-shaming, or unlawful treatment of tenants.

The platform should focus on:

**Education + Documentation + Community + Resources + Advocacy + Professionalism**

# 2. CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Follow these principles:

### SIMPLICITY FIRST

A landlord should understand what the organization does within approximately 5 seconds of landing on the homepage.

Do not overwhelm visitors with dozens of menu items.

### PREMIUM, NOT CORPORATE-BORING

Avoid the visual style of:

* Government websites

Think:

**Apple-level simplicity + Stripe-level clarity + modern professional association credibility.**

* Role-based access control
* Least-privilege permissions

* Encryption in transit
* Secure backups
* Audit logging
* Rate limiting
* Brute-force protection
* Bot protection
* CSRF protection
* XSS protection
* SQL injection protection
* Secure file uploads

* Automated backups

* Security monitoring and alerting
